reproductions. Each card names its source, date, institution and licence in the
game.

## Usage notes

- Images are stored locally in `public/archive/` and used as card backgrounds.
- Patterns are authored interpretations; do not represent them as exact museum
  reproductions.
- If a source is reused outside this project, re-check the current licence and
  credit the institution and photographer where required.
- The `Daily Weave` card is generated from the date; it has no archive image or
  provenance line.

original transcriptions of traditional or public-domain tunes; verify local
